Who I Am

I’m Rafael Widjajahakim — a data scientist in training with over a decade of hands-on work in biomedical research and pharmaceutical market research. I’m currently completing my Master of Science in Data Science at Boston University while bridging the gap between clinical domain expertise and modern machine learning.

My path is a bit unconventional: I started in wet-lab research, moved into data-heavy clinical trial work, pivoted to pharma secondary research and analytics, and now I’m focused on building end-to-end data science skills that can make a real difference in health outcomes.

Experience & Background

  • Biomedical Research — ~10 years of experience across ophthalmology clinical trials (AREDS), lipid research, and real-world evidence (RWE) studies
  • Pharma Market Research — Line-of-therapy analyses, executive summaries, and dashboard reporting for pharmaceutical clients
  • Data Science Internship (6 months) — AWS EC2, Snowflake, Python-based data cleaning and pipeline work in a production environment
  • Teaching Assistantship / Coursework — Graduate-level modules in statistical modeling, machine learning, and data engineering
