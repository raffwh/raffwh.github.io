## Who I Am

I'm Rafael Widjajahakim — a data scientist in training with over a decade of hands-on work in biomedical research and pharmaceutical market research. I'm currently completing my **Master of Science in Data Science at Boston University** while bridging the gap between clinical domain expertise and modern machine learning.
